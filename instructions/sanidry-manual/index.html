Step one

Get to know your dryer

Connect the power cord from your USB-C adapter to the dryer. The indicator light turns blue when it's connected correctly. Then choose your drying cycle, temperature and UV cleaning.

1

Dryer cycle

Choose a 2-hour cycle or an 8-hour overnight cycle.

2

Temperature

Select low (up to 40 ℃) or high (up to 50 ℃) heat to dry and dehumidify.

3

UV cleaning

A 5-minute double UV-C cycle finishes the job — the ultimate clean for your IEMs.

Fit and finish

Place your in-ear monitors inside the cavity, close the lid and press Dryer to begin. The unit shuts off automatically when the cycle finishes, and runs quietly enough to leave overnight.

Technology

How it works

1. Constant, controlled heating

The dryer manages its own combination of time and temperature. Heat from the heating circuit is carried through the air into the cavity, gently driving moisture out of your in-ear monitors.

2. UV-C sterilisation

Two LED UV tubes generate ultraviolet light at 260–280 nm, killing surface bacteria and viruses. The twin-tube layout gives broader, more even exposure across the cavity.

Sweat and earwax build-up doesn't just smell — it breeds bacteria and degrades the drivers and filters. A few minutes of drying after each use is the cheapest repair you'll ever make.

Care

Everyday care

  • Dry your IEMs after every session — especially in hot or humid conditions.

  • Wipe earwax from the sound bore with the cleaning tool before placing them in the dryer — the dryer removes moisture, not wax.

  • Remove the cable or connector module where your model allows it, so heat can circulate around the mould.

  • Keep the dryer on a dry, level surface away from water, and use only the supplied or a reputable USB-C power adapter.

If something's not right

Troubleshooting

No indicator light when plugged in

Check that the USB-C connector is fully seated at both ends and that your adapter is powered. Try another adapter or cable — the indicator turns blue only once the dryer is receiving power.

The dryer won't start a cycle

Confirm the lid is closed and the unit is connected to power. Press Dryer once for a 2-hour cycle; the light will indicate the cycle has begun.

My IEMs are still damp after a cycle

Use the 8-hour cycle and the high temperature setting in humid conditions, and make sure the moulds aren't nested tightly together. Very wet devices may need a second cycle.

Can I dry other electronics?

Yes — SaniDry is suitable for earphones, drivercomm units, in-ear monitors and other small electronics that tolerate gentle warmth of up to 50 ℃. Don't put in items that are wet with liquid water, solvents or chemicals.

Is UV-C light safe?

The UV-C light operates only inside the closed cavity. Don't look into the cavity while the cycle is running, and don't attempt to run the UV cycle with the lid open.
